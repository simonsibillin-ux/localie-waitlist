What is Localie

One place for every
trusted local service

Finding a reliable tradie has always been a guessing game. Localie changes that. We've built a curated marketplace where every single provider — from electricians to dog groomers — has been independently verified before they can appear. No randoms. No risk. Just locals you can trust.

Why we're different

Not everyone gets in.

Unlike other platforms where anyone can sign up, every Localie provider passes a rigorous verification process before a single customer sees them.

ABN verified
Every provider's Australian Business Number is confirmed before approval. No exceptions.
Insurance checked
Current insurance certificates are collected and verified. If it lapses, they come off the platform.
Reputation reviewed
We review track record and reputation as part of every application. Quality is the baseline.
